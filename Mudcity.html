Mudcity

32 minutes · 2K DCP · 2012 · Undergraduate Thesis Project

Filmed on the road, without any permits, over a period of five hot summer days, Mudcity is the story of a young, nameless vagabond who, after finding a box of photographs and cassette tapes in a recently deceased woman's home, decides to seek out the deceased woman's son.
