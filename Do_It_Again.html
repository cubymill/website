Do It Again

8.5 minutes · 2K DCP · 2018

Do It Again combines three moments of shared looking at Chicago's Marina City; the climactic scene from Buzz Kulik's The Hunter, a re-enactment for an All-State Commercial, and a tight-rope walk of Nik Wallenda.
